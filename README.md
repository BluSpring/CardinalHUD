# CardinalHUD
A (weirdly enough, Vanilla+) custom cardinal/compass HUD that also replaces the locator bar, because I needed a waypoint system.

What are the waypoints, you may ask? Easy: lodestones!
Configurable to be held, hotbar or in inventory, you can use the Vanilla compasses as your waypoints!

Feel free to rename it too for extra familiarity! Colours not included, though.
